Main Purpose

The main purpose of our website is to make people have an open minded thoughts—to make our country a democratic country whereas people will understand each other's opinion—for them to not be bias, prejudice, close minded or for them to show no discrimination on each other, for them to have a basis on every opinion that they want to say.

Our sub-purpose is for them to learn more about politics, laws and news in the Philippines. And increase global awareness amongst people.
